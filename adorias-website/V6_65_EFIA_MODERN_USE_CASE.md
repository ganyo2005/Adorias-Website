## Main correction
Wedding, sports, portrait and creator questions now route through a modern use-case advisor before the older generic
diagnosis paths. Wedding photography explicitly prefers interchangeable-lens camera bodies and rewards mirrorless/DSLR,
full-frame, autofocus, hybrid video and dual-card evidence where those features are actually documented. Camcorders are
de-prioritized for photo/hybrid wedding requests rather than repeatedly dominating recommendations.

## Diversity
Efia keeps a short recommendation history so the same few SKUs do not automatically recur. It also limits excessive
same-brand concentration in a three-option answer, while still allowing two products from one brand when evidence supports it.

## Readability
Recommendations now render as clearly separated blocks:
camera name → WHY IT WORKS → KEEP IN MIND, followed by separate ESSENTIALS and OPTIONAL / NICE TO HAVE sections.
Line breaks are preserved in the chat UI.
