## What changed
Efia can now respond to requests such as “build me a complete church livestream setup” or “what else do I need?”
by moving beyond a single camera recommendation and constructing a cross-category Adorias solution.

The builder starts with the camera/video result from the precision diagnosis engine, then considers lenses, power,
tripod/support, audio, live-production hardware, cables/connectivity and memory/storage. It labels items as essential
or workflow-dependent.

## Compatibility protection
Lens and battery/charger suggestions use stronger guards. If the selected camera record does not contain enough evidence
to prove the mount or battery relationship, Efia produces a VERIFY BEFORE SALE warning rather than guessing an accessory.

## Workflow questions
Before treating a system as final, Efia asks for camera count, shooting distance, lighting conditions, audio-source count,
existing switcher/capture equipment, cable-run lengths and whether streaming is computer-based or standalone.

## Current commercial limitation
The builder still does not claim a setup is inside a customer's budget until dependable current Adorias selling prices
are connected to catalogue records.
