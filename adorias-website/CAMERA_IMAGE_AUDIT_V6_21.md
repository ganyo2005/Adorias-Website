# Adorias Camera Image Re-Audit — V6.21

All 55 camera-body records have been reset to a fresh audit state. An old image URL is no longer treated as proof that the picture is correct.

## New publishing rule

- Prefer the exact camera with its confirmed marketed kit lens when that kit is a real configuration.
- If no confirmed kit configuration is established, use an exact body-only image.
- Blackmagic Pocket Cinema cameras are treated as body-only unless Adorias explicitly sells a lens bundle.
- Similar-looking bodies are not interchangeable.
- Manufacturer product imagery is preferred; third-party imagery must be visually/model-name verified before it is considered approved.

## Research checks used in this pass

- Sony officially markets Alpha 7 III / ILCE-7M3M with the 28–70mm zoom lens.
- Nikon officially documents D90 + 18–105mm and D3500 + 18–55mm kit configurations; current Z bodies also have multiple official kit options.
- Panasonic officially lists GH5 and GH6 lens kits, and documents GH5 II/GH6 kit variants.
- Blackmagic's official specifications show the Pocket Cinema Camera bodies and list lens-mount dust caps rather than an included lens.
