# Canon official-image rebuild policy — V6.26

All third-party Canon camera hotlinks were removed.

From this version onward:
1. Canon camera imagery must originate from Canon's official product/store/media pages.
2. Kit imagery is used only where Canon itself documents that kit configuration.
3. A camera may remain temporarily image-less rather than display a third-party, broken, or questionable asset.
4. Product cards now open a full detail view. The detail view supports multiple images (`images[]`) so official Canon gallery views can be added as they are approved.
5. The detail view exposes catalogue details, compatibility, battery/power information when present, cart action, and WhatsApp enquiry.
